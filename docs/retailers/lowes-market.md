## Upload & setup (owned by DOC) — codesheet driven

- Retrieve the generic codesheet from the Lowe's Market drive: **`UPDATED-Lowe's Market Codesheet.csv`**.
- Flyer run → codesheet upload; use that week's PDF base directory as the base path.
- **Troubleshooting codesheet errors:** open the codesheet and the error; check whether the named pages exist in the FTP.
  - If pages don't exist → email the retailer for the files.
  - If pages exist → check that the codesheet name matches the file name and update the codesheet to match.

### Setup QC checklist
- Mark off flyer creation; check flyer-page dates match the run; check the geography tab for missing stores (flag to full-time team); complete setup QC.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items

- **White space on pages:** previews may show large white spaces requiring re-slicing of those pages (same process as Food King — see the risk-item video). Check every page for large white spaces during pre-FQC and fix before FQC.

## QC specifics

- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF):** **include coupons**; exclude packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks. Coupons/embedded promotions boxed and tagged as an item (promotion in sale story, details in disclaimer).
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag OFF):** **SIMP POP retailer — Tag QC is not completed.** Include name/brand, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price. **Exclude SKU and URLs.**
  - Bold text = Name; unbolded text after = Description. Prices as on the flyer (prefix, current, postfix, original).
  - Page-level callouts apply to all items on the page (e.g. sale story `-50¢/EA digital coupon`; disclaimer `Limit 2 Items Per Coupon; Limit 1 Coupon Per Customer`).
  - Categories chart: Toys, Seasonal, Pharmacy, Pets, Outdoor Living, Home Essentials, Health & Beauty, Grocery, Grills, Gifts, Coupon, Beverages, Beer/Wine & Liquor. Every item needs a category.
- **Image QC:** N/A. Spotchecks: confirm the correct option matches the page.

## Post-processing & FQC (owned by DOC)

- Item Category QC: ensure items have a Google category.
- Ad-hoc / pre-FQC: Standard 4 thumbnails; no theme applied; geography tab (flag missing stores); check pages for large white spaces (fix per risk item).
- Complete the FQC checklist.
