## Files & schedule
- **Files arrive:** Monday (sent by email, usually a Dropbox link).
- **Cadence:** Available/Valid From Friday → Available/Valid To Thursday.
- Preview: ad hoc. Linking document: No.

## Upload & setup (owned by Flex)
- Download individual pages, rename them by date, upload into SFTP.
  - **Do NOT download the main combined PDF for Fadmin to split** — that produces text errors on page 1.
- Manually upload pages → Auto-Group.
- Create Pricing Zone: **1 PZ = base = EN**; add all stores.
- Check that page 1's callout is not covered and text is clear.
- Complete Setup QC checklist.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items
- **Page 1 callout obscured / text errors** — do not split the combined PDF in Fadmin; upload the individual pages instead.

## QC specifics
- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF):** box each item individually. **Exclude** coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag ON):** Brand tag/QC specific. Include name, valid dates, description, SKU, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price, URLs; **exclude pre/postfix.** (Doc lists PDF image auto-selection inconsistently — treat as standard.)
- **Spotchecks:** check all items are boxed.

## FQC (owned by Flex)
- Confirm dates match PDF; **no duplicate titles on the cover page**, no errors, everything visible/clear.
- Confirm availability toggles; thumbnails (4 Standard) include retailer logo.
- All items boxed and tagged; spotchecks complete (20% of PZs); previews published/clickable; sessions completed; geography correct.
- **Flyer Review type: Lite.**

## Out-of-processing
- **Page Swap:** standard baseline page swap.
