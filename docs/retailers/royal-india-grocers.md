## Files & schedule

- **Files received:** Monday
- **Available From:** Friday · **Valid From:** Thursday · **Available To:** Thursday · **Valid To:** Friday
- **Workflow:** Upload & Setup (FLEX, 4 days out) → FQC (FLEX, 1 day out).

## Upload & setup (owned by DOC; QC by FLEX)

- **Manual upload:** Pages Tab → Edit → select all pages from the SFTP menu (folder named after live/valid date) → Confirm & Upload.
- Once pages are listed, Auto-Group or manually add page numbers into the Grouping Number field.
  - **Brampton page always goes last.**
  - **⚠️ Note:** the Brampton page is sometimes sent later than the base pages — **flag if not present**.
- Save & Confirm; **do NOT Process Internally.**
- **Pricing zone creation:** Base zone = Mississauga store; Brampton zone = Brampton store.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items (retailer-specific)

- **Brampton page** must be present, in the **last position**, and available **only for the Brampton location**. It is sometimes sent later than base pages — flag if missing.
- When uploading from SFTP, confirm no pages remain in the SFTP that weren't uploaded.

## QC specifics

### Box Draw (Low complexity; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF)
- No linking document required.
- **Exclude** coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.

### Tag / Tag QC (Low complexity; Auto-tag ON)
- No linking document required.
- **Include:** name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, price, sale story, disclaimer, original price. **Exclude** SKU, categories, URLs.
- Standard pricing spotchecks in pipeline.

## Pre-Final QC / FQC (owned by FLEX)
- Confirm dates against PDF (usually first or last page); availability toggles (available everywhere); thumbnails correct incl. retailer logo (4 standard).
- Standard flyer review checks: all items boxed/tagged; spotchecks complete (20% of pricing zones); previews published & clickable; sessions completed; geography correct.
- Confirm the Brampton page (if included) is in the last position and only for the Brampton location.
