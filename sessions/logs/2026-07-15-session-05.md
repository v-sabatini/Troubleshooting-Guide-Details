# Session Log — 2026-07-15 (Session 05)

**Goal:** Read/learn the retailer **OneGuide** documents in Confluence and build
retailer-specific processing instructions for the bot.

## What we found

- The **OneGuide** lives in Confluence space **VEN (Vendor)**, page `10823336187`
  — but it's an **A–Z directory that links out to one Google Doc per
  retailer/banner**. The real content is in **Google Drive**, not Confluence.
- **493 unique retailer Google Docs.** Each follows a consistent, rich template:
  contacts → account info (segment/tier, publication cadence, Slack, hosted URL)
  → historical/issue log → processing instructions per flyer type (files, cadence,
  preview, processing type, involvement, day-by-day workflow) → upload & setup
  (codesheet steps + **retailer-specific Common Errors**) → QC (box/tag
  include-exclude, category charts, image QC/risk items) → post-processing → FQC →
  flyer review → live dates → out-of-processing.
- ⚠️ **Docs contain credentials and contacts** — e.g. ALDI's guide includes a
  file-transfer password and staff emails, with a "vendors should never contact
  external members" warning.

## Decisions made (from Vanessa)

- **Scope:** convert **all 493**, **phased** alphabetically across sessions.
- **Contacts:** **omit contacts entirely** (bot points to the OneGuide for who to
  contact). **Credentials/passwords are always stripped** regardless.

## What we built

- `sources/oneguide-retailer-index.md` — the full **493-retailer directory**
  (name → Google Doc) + the standard OneGuide structure + handling notes.
- `docs/retailers/_TEMPLATE.md` — retailer article template.
- `docs/retailers/README.md` — folder guide + **progress tracker** + how-to-continue.
- **First 6 retailer guides converted** (batch 1): ALDI, The Beer Store, 2001
  Audio Video, Academy Sports + Outdoors, Accès Pharma, Ace Hardware — each with
  account facts, cadence, upload/codesheet specifics (config names + toggles),
  box/tag rules, retailer-specific common errors/risk items, and FQC notes.
  Contacts/credentials omitted.
- Wired into `docs/00-index.md`, `README`-level status, and CURRENT-STATUS.

## Notes / observations

- Guides range from **Simple/Lite** (e.g. The Beer Store, Accès Pharma) to
  **complex** (Ace Hardware = High box-draw complexity with detailed price-tagging
  rules; ABSCo/Albertsons = large multi-banner). Article length scales accordingly.
- The retailer "Common Errors" and "Risk Items" sections are especially valuable
  for the troubleshooting bot.
- Reading each full Doc is context-heavy → the phased, batch-per-session approach
  is the right call. ~6–12 per session is a comfortable pace.
