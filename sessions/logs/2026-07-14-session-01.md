## Decisions made

- **Article structure** is standardized: symptom → likely cause → fix →
  escalate → source. Exact error strings kept **verbatim** (users paste them).
- **One self-contained article per topic** to suit NotebookLM's per-source
  retrieval.
- Every article **cites its source**; provenance tracked in `source-map.md`.
- Prototype lives as **Markdown in GitHub**; NotebookLM migration is a later,
  documented step (`NOTEBOOKLM.md`).
