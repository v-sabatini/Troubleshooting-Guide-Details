## Files & schedule

- **Files received:** Monday.
- **Publication cadence:** Available From Tuesday → Available To Monday; Valid From Tuesday → Valid To Tuesday.

## Upload & setup (owned by DOC)

- **Manual upload from SFTP:** upload as English pages (generally 2); auto-group or manually assign. One Pricing Zone = "Base"; assign all stores from the store set (just 1).

### Setup QC (owned by Flex)
- Edit details: Valid Wednesday to Tuesday, same Available dates.
- Toggles: **no toggles checked for the Weekly (available everywhere)**; **Hide in distribution & Flipp for the Seasonal**.
- No theme. Check Sessions and Vendor Tasks.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items (retailer-specific)

- **Look for multiple products** on an item.
- Items without a brand name must be tagged as **"Debranded"** on the Brand and Name tab.

## QC specifics

### Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot ON)
- No linking document for Box.
- **Include:** special weblinks. **Exclude:** coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media.
- **Box the entirety of Page 1** — it becomes a link leading to their homepage.

### Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag OFF)
- Linking Document required (Tag/QC-specific).
- **Include:** name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, SKU, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price, Brand. **Exclude:** URLs.
- Brand goes in the Brand tab; items without a brand = "Debranded". Enter all SKUs (items with multiple SKUs → all in the SKU tab). Tag each item to its specific category and select Google categories.

### Image QC
- **Cutouts only.**

## Out-of-processing
- **Page swaps are very common** (a few throughout the week). The retailer emails item-update requests and sends new files via SFTP; the Flex team can action.
