## Files & schedule

- **Files received:** Monday.
- **Publication:** Available From Monday; Valid Tuesday → Tuesday.
- **Linking document:** No.

## Upload & setup (owned by DOC)

- Manual upload; available everywhere; flyer type "Flyer".
- **EN pricing zone gets all English pages; FR pricing zone gets all French pages.**
- Confirm flyer pages look the same as the provided PDF.

### ⚠️ Common errors / risk items (retailer-specific)

- **Box QC — some boxes missed.** Box every individual item.

## QC specifics

- **Box Draw — Low complexity. Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF. No linking doc.** Exclude coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks. **Box every individual item; box separately when items have different product numbers, prices, or product names.**
- **Tag / Tag QC — Low. Auto-tag OFF. PDF image auto-selection ON. Valid dates and URLs excluded.** Include brand, name, pre/postfix, description, SKU, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price.
- **Image QC:** use a clean PDF whenever possible; watch for PDFs with no clean background and switch to cutout.

## FQC / go-live (owned by Vendor)

- Legibility heights 40×30; images used are PDFs.
- **Add URLs to banners with no CTA (PRO only):** local.bumpertobumper.ca/en/store and /fr/magasins-de-pieces-auto.
- **Mark In-Store Only.**
- **Flyer Review type: Lite.**
