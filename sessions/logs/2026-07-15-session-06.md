retailer guides into `docs/retailers/`.

## Method (for future refreshes)

- Index: `sources/oneguide-retailer-index.md` (retailer → Google Doc).
- Slugs: Unicode-normalized (lowercase, accents stripped, `&`→"and", apostrophes
  removed, non-alnum → hyphen).
- Process: chunk remaining retailers (13/agent), dispatch general-purpose
  subagents with `AGENT_INSTRUCTIONS.md`, leak-scan, commit per wave. Recompute
  "remaining" from files on disk between waves to avoid drift.
