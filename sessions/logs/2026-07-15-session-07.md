## Key facts learned (V2)

- V1↔V2 is ~1:1 **except** flyer items (one V2 **Offer** ↔ many V1 flyer items);
  new content (DVM, NativeX EU/SA) has **only** V2 ids.
- **Nexus API** = single ingestion entry point / source of truth; **Curator** =
  single V2 serving source; **Flyers-NG** keeps legacy App/Web/Hosted working via a
  V2→V1 converter during transition.
- Per-channel + per-section/zone distribution is a first-class V2 concept — the
  backdrop to the hosted-only / single-zone publish gaps.
- These are **strategy/architecture** docs, not ops runbooks — the "how to re-run
  V2 publishing" mechanics are still undocumented (GAP-001/002 remain open).
