## Files & schedule

- **Files received:** Monday (retailer emails once files are dropped; email contains flyer dates — confirm receipt).
- **Publication cadence:** Available/Valid Friday–Thursday.

## Upload & setup (owned by FLEX)

- **Manual upload.** Overview → Edit Details → Show/hide rarely-used fields → ensure **Height = 4096**.
- Pages → Edit → use the small-case folder to upload Page 1 and 2 (delivered as one combined PDF; FADMIN splits into individual pages) → Upload → Auto Group → Save & Complete.
- Flyer creation: one pricing zone `Base`, add both pages in order, Save & Done. Add all stores.
- Setup QC: confirm pages uploaded and flyer dates; thumbnails Standard 4; preview dates set.

## QC specifics

- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF):** exclude coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag OFF):** include brand, name, description, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price. **Exclude** pre/postfix, valid dates, SKU, URLs.
- **Image QC:** **all images are cutouts.**
- No category QC, no URLs, no SKUs, no ad-hoc QC, no out-of-processing tasks.

## FQC / flyer review

- Final QC (FLEX): Pricing Zone tabs — check item boxes; Vertical & Horizontal preview; **leg heights 60/40**; Standard thumbnails.
- **Flyer Review type: Lite** — dates match PDF; Available Everywhere; pagination order; item boxes; iframe; vertical geography; no stores/FSAs added or removed.
