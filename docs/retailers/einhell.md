## Files & schedule

- **When files arrive / cadence:** ad hoc. **Linking document:** sent via email — **one for English, one for French.**

## Upload & setup (owned by FLEX)

- Pages may need to be added to the SFTP by the processor if the client sends files over email.
- **Manual upload:** Pages Tab → Edit → select all SFTP pages → Confirm & Upload → Auto-Group (or enter grouping numbers); ensure correct language selected → Save & Confirm. **Do NOT Process Internally.**
- **Pricing zones:** create **English** (select all applicable pages) and **French**. Stores: **add all stores to English; add QC stores to French.**
- **Setup QC:** confirm all pages uploaded (RISK — check none remain in SFTP); confirm dates; thumbnails Standard 4; preview dates set.

### ⚠️ Common errors / risk items (retailer-specific)

- **Look for multiple products** in one image/block.

## QC specifics

- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF).** Linking doc required (shared Box/Tag). **Include** packaged deals and the **retailer logo**. **Exclude** coupons, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag ON).** Linking doc required. Include name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, SKU, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price, URLs.
  - **Brand: ALWAYS "EINHELL"** on every item.
  - **Name:** black, ALL CAPS text (e.g. "18V Cordless Deluxe Handheld/Stick Vacuum").
  - **Description:** red text or anything other than Name & SKU (e.g. the "Model Number" from the tagging doc, e.g. "TE-HV 18/06 Li").
  - **SKU:** the black number (e.g. "2347195"), also in the tagging document.
- **Image QC:** PDF preferred if clean; otherwise cutouts accepted.

## Final QC / go-live

- **Pre-FQC (DOC):** confirm dates vs. PDF; availability toggles; thumbnails correct incl. retailer logo. Standard checks: all items boxed/tagged, spotchecks (20% of pricing zones), previews published & clickable, sessions complete, geography correct.
- **Flyer Review type: Lite** (owned by FLEX).
