## Files & schedule
- **Files received:** Monday. The SpartanNash contact drops files **and a Posting Document** to the FTP on Mondays and emails when files are dropped.
- **Publication:** Available From Saturday → Available To Saturday (one-day preview); **Valid From Sunday → Valid To Saturday.**
- **Preview date:** the Thursday before go-live (e.g. Saturday Oct 12 live → preview Thursday Oct 10).
- No linking document.

## Upload & setup (owned by Flex)
- Download the Posting Document to confirm page order; upload pages from FTP and order per the Posting Document.
- **Pricing zone "Base"; add all stores (1 store — 3077).** Wait for sessions; ensure vendor tasks active.
- Confirm Page 1 valid dates are Sunday–Saturday.
- Edit Details: Available Sat–Sat (one-day preview); Valid Sun–Sat; **hidden in hosted**; preview date = Thursday before go-live; no theme. Thumbnails Standard 4; leg heights 45/35.
- **Clear the FTP files (including the Posting Document) after setup.**

## Box Draw (Low — Auto-Box OFF, Box QC bot OFF)
- **Include:** coupons, packaged deals. **Exclude:** retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.

## Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag OFF)
- **Include:** name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price. **Exclude:** SKU, URLs. Brand used for Box/Tag.
- **Name:** include all items offered in the ad block. **Prefix** from the approved dropdown.
- **Multiple prices:** put the sale price in **Current Price** and the coupon/extra-savings offer in **Sale Story** — **do NOT put the second price in the Original Price field.**
- **Sale Story:** tag Extra Savings / MFR Rebate / Digital Coupon banners as shown.
- **Valid dates:** watch for **2-Day / 3-Day sale banners** — tag all applicable items with the unique valid dates.
- **Categories:** every item; Baby → Health and Beauty; Pet → Grocery.

## Image QC
- **NOT using PDF images — use item cutouts ONLY.**

## Pre-FQC / FQC (owned by Vendor)
- Complete remaining spotchecks; check narrow pages in Storefront Spotcheck per pricing zone (merge to next page), mark Autostack Spotcheck complete; image QC not required (no client prefs given); **review each page for special days (3-Day Sales) and apply correct valid dates.**
- FQC checklist: spot checks; thumbnails Standard 4; legibility heights; Geography matches previous weeks; pages in correct order/region; toggles correct; all items tagged; dates match PDF; no real warnings on overview.
- **Flyer Review type: Lite.**
