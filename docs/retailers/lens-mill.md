## Files & schedule

- **Files received:** Monday (dropped into FTP).
- **Cadence:** Available/Valid From Monday, Available/Valid To Sunday. **May not always be weekly** — can be 2–3 week runs, so double-check dates against the PDF.
- **Processing type:** Auto Stack.

## Upload & setup (owned by Flex)

- Manual upload all pages → set all EN → Auto-group.
- 1 pricing zone = "base", all pages.
- Add all stores.

### Setup QC checklist
- Edit Details: Available/Valid From Monday, Available/Valid To Sunday (match PDF dates).
- Hidden on hosted; No theme.
- All stores added.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items

- **Valid From date is not on the flyer.** It is the upcoming **Monday after the previous flyer ends** (e.g. if the previous flyer ends Sunday the 12th, this flyer starts Monday the 13th). Compare against the Flyer Runs page to confirm.

## QC specifics

- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot ON):** exclude coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag ON):** include brand, name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price. **Exclude SKU and URLs.** Standard tagging rules apply (example: Name "OUTDOOR PILLOWS & CUSHIONS", Prefix "STARTING AT", Current Price 9.99, Postfix "EACH").
- **Image selection:** clean images are **not** available for this merchant — it will always be a cutout.
- Spotchecks: standard rules.

## FQC / flyer review

- **Final QC (owned by Flex):** QC thumbnails; Geography — no change week over week (flag any changes to the FT team immediately).
- **Flyer Review type: Lite** (owned by Vendor).
- No post-processing tasks.
