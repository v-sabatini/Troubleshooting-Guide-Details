## Files & schedule
- **Files received:** ad hoc (client may send via email — pages may need to be added to the SFTP by the processor). Internal previews sent to CP. Linking document used for Tag.

## Upload & setup (owned by Flex; bilingual EN/FR)
- **Manual upload:** Pages > Edit > select all pages > Confirm & Upload > Auto-Group or manually enter grouping numbers. **Ensure correct language per page (some EN, some FR).** Save & Confirm.
- **Pricing zones:** create an English PZ (select applicable pages), then a French PZ; add all applicable stores/FSAs.
- **Add the linking document to all tasks.**

### Setup QC
- Confirm all pages uploaded (Pricing Zone > Items View). **Risk: if uploading from SFTP, confirm no pages in the SFTP were left un-uploaded.**
- Confirm flyer dates (usually first/last page); Thumbnails Standard 4; ensure all preview dates set.

## QC specifics
- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF; linking doc required):** exclude retailer logo, sign-up, social, special weblinks. Box all callouts & items; also box all **CTA buttons** (Shop Now, Learn More, etc.).
- **Tag / Tag QC (Medium; Auto-tag ON; linking doc required):** include Name, Pre/Postfix, Valid Dates, Description, SKU, Price, Sale Story, Categories, Disclaimer, Original Price, URLs; Brand used for both Box/Tag. **Brand, item names, descriptions, URLs, product size (pre-price text) and disclaimers come from the linking document — copy & paste into the tag interface.** For CTA buttons, use the corresponding product URL and change Item Type to **Link**.
- **Image QC:** PDF preferred if clean, otherwise cutouts accepted.

## Post-processing (DOC)
- **Pre-FQC:** confirm dates (per PDF), availability toggles, thumbnails (incl. retailer logo). **Descriptions, URLs, and Disclaimers must not be empty — update if they are.** Confirm all boxed/tagged, spotchecks done (20% of pricing zones), previews published/clickable, sessions completed, geography correct.
- **FQC:** complete checklist. **Send preview links in the RCC Slack channel** for the team to review — Overview > AdHoc Processing > Preview link; copy links to Slack. Revisions will be sent to complete ahead of go-live.

## Flyer review
- **Type: Lite.** Checks: flyer dates, sessions complete, previews correct, all items tagged, geography correct, availability toggles correct.
