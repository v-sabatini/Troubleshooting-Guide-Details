## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items (retailer-specific)
- **Ad-hoc inserts:** the retailer usually sends an insert ~a day before they want it live. Inserts are **not boxed/tagged** except for **one direct link to the badboy.com homepage**. The insert always goes in **position 1** of the flyer — set a trigger to add it on the requested date and another trigger to remove it on the requested removal date.

## QC specifics
- **Box Draw (Low):** **Include** sign-up page, special weblinks, retailer logo, packaged deals. **Exclude** social media, coupons. Linking document required for tagging and item QC.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low):** Include all fields (brand, name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, SKU, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price, URLs). Tag every field **as it appears on the PDF**.
- **Image QC:** no specific item examples were filled in.
