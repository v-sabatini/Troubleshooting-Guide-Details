## Upload & setup (owned by FLEX)

- **⚠️ Pages may need to be added to the SFTP by the processor if the client sends files over email.**
- **Manual upload:** Pages Tab → Edit → select all pages from the SFTP menu → Confirm & Upload. Auto-Group or manually add page numbers; ensure correct language selected. Save & Confirm; **do NOT Process Internally.**
- **Pricing zone creation:** create 1 pricing zone = base; select all applicable pages; Save & Confirm; add all stores.

### Setup QC
- Confirm all pages uploaded (Pricing Zone Tab → Items View); **RISK:** if uploading from SFTP, confirm no un-uploaded pages remain.
- Confirm flyer dates (usually first or last page); complete 4 Standard thumbnails; ensure all preview dates set.

## QC specifics

### Box Draw (Low complexity; Auto-Box OFF, Box QC bot OFF)
- No linking document required.
- **Exclude** coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- All items boxed separately.

### Tag / Tag QC (Low complexity; Auto-tag OFF)
- No linking document required.
- **Include:** name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price. **Exclude** SKU and URLs.
- Standard pricing spotchecks in pipeline.

### Image QC (owned by FLEX)
- Standard Image QC — PDF preferred if clean; otherwise cutouts accepted.

## Pre-Final QC / FQC (owned by FLEX/DOC)
- Confirm dates against PDF; availability toggles; thumbnails incl. retailer logo.
- Standard flyer review checks: all items boxed/tagged; spotchecks complete (20% of pricing zones); previews published & clickable; sessions completed; geography correct.

## Flyer review
- **Flyer Review type: Lite** (owned by FLEX). Checks: flyer dates match PDF; toggles available on all platforms; pagination in chronological order; vertical preview showing correctly (all offers interactive); all items tagged accurately; geography no change WoW.
