## Files & schedule

- **When files arrive:** Ad-hoc. **They do not use the SFTP — the client emails files.**
- **Publication cadence:** Available From Thursday, Valid From Thursday; Available To Saturday, Valid To Saturday. **Confirm dates using the PDF pages.**
- **Linking document:** N/A.
- **Processing type:** Auto-stack.

## Upload & setup (owned by Flex)

- **Manually upload the pages.**
- Create one pricing zone — "base".
- Ensure all stores are added.
- Available and valid dates are the same.
- Complete the Setup QC checklist.

## QC specifics

### Box Draw (Low complexity — Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot OFF; owned by Vendor)
- **Include:** packaged deals.
- **Exclude:** coupons, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- Items and their offers should be individually boxed; coupon items should also be boxed.
- **If one image covers multiple price points (e.g. dog food sizes), box the image and place text boxes on the offers.**

### Tag / Tag QC (Low complexity — Auto-tag ON, PDF image auto-selection ON; owned by Vendor)
- Brand is Tag/QC specific.
- **Include:** name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, SKU, price, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price.
- **Exclude:** URLs.

### Image QC
- Choose the white-background PDF where possible; otherwise choose cutout.

### Spotchecks
- N/A.

## Post-processing (owned by DOC)

- Mark autostack spotcheck complete; check if sessions need to be run; confirm boxed and tagged; check dates.
- No theme. Draw standard four thumbnails.
- **Item Category QC:** page categories on all pages except the first (1 per page).
- Complete FQC checklist.
