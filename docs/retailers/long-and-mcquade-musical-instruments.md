## Files & schedule

- **Cadence:** Ad hoc / monthly.
- **Preview:** send preview to the retailer at least **5 business days before launch**; they have 2 days to send revisions, Flipp has 2 days to implement before launch.

## Upload & setup

**Upload (Catalogue, flyer type 3843):**
1. PDFs sent via SFTP; download the links spreadsheet to local drive.
2. Upload pages manually; set EN pages English and FR pages French.
3. Auto Group → Save & Complete.

**Setup QC:**
1. Create 2 pricing zones — **EN + FR**; add all stores to both.
2. Attach the links spreadsheet to Vendor Tag and Vendor Tag QC.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items

- **Links:** 95% of products should have links assigned. At Pre-FQC, the "Items without a URL" count should be **less than 5%** of total items.

## QC specifics

- **Box Draw (Low):** no coupons, sign-up page, special weblinks, retailer logo, packaged deals, social media. Linking document required for tagging and item QC.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low):** include name, pre/postfix, valid dates, description, SKU, price, categories, sale story, original price, URLs. **Exclude brand and disclaimer.**
  - Name: UPPER CASE. Description: the smaller/lighter font in the title.
  - SKU tagged as seen on the PDF. URLs tagged as seen in the linking document.

## Pre-Final QC / out-of-process

- QC thumbnails; Items without URL < 5%; Edit Details (dates vs email, preview date ≥ 5 business days before launch, available everywhere, external run name set, theme set).
- Pricing Zones: item view — every product with a price/sales story has a box; vertical preview — link redirect visible; page order chronological.
- Geography: no missing/added stores.
- Once FQC'd, send preview to the contact for review/revisions (5-business-day window as above).
- **Page swaps:** generic page-swap process.
