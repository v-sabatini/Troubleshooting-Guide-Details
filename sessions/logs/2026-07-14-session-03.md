  resolve to **`MSC-####`** (Marketing Science) — verified via `getJiraIssue
  PIA-8847` → `MSC-8847`. So the three real destinations are **FD**, **CLSD**,
  and **MSC**.
- **Sampled each board** (created ≥ 2024-01-01) and computed real **turnaround**
  (resolved − created):

    High volume (~100 tickets in the first 24 days of 2024). Turnaround very
    spread: ~31% ≤3 days, ~49% ≤1 week, but a long tail (~23% >30 days).
  - **CLSD** — "Content Layer Service Desk", the content-platform engineering
    desk. Mostly **Investigations (54%)** (categorization gaps, duplicate
    publication IDs, cloning/**WES** errors, harmonization fixes, tagging bugs),
    plus **Asks/Tasks**. Turnaround: ~44% ≤3 days, ~62% ≤1 week; ~20% still open.
  - **MSC** — "Marketing Science" (the analytics **"PIA"** function). Mostly
    **Foursquare AAAS measurement** + store-trip reporting; slower,
    reporting-paced (mostly 1–4 weeks).

  map, what each board does, turnaround expectations, and how to write a good

## Decisions made

- **Sampling, not full ingest.** FD and CLSD are large, high-volume engineering
  boards; MSC is mostly unrelated advertising work. Full ingest would be noise, so
  we sampled to characterize purpose + turnaround and were **transparent** that
  numbers are sample-based estimates, not SLAs.
- Kept the post-escalation content as **one combined article** (it's reference
  material about the flow, not a per-error troubleshooting guide).

## Key facts worth remembering

- **`PIA-####` == `MSC-####`** (Marketing Science). No separate PIA project.
- **FD** = broken-indexer fixes (missing flyers). **CLSD** = content-platform
  engineering catch-all (incl. harmonization fixes, cloning/WES, categorization).
  **MSC** = Foursquare measurement / store-trip reporting.
- Rough turnaround: FD & CLSD often within a few days but with real tails; MSC
  runs on a reporting cadence (weeks).
