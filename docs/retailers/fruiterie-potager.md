## Files & schedule

## Upload & setup (owned by Vendor)
- Manual upload: **EN pages for the EN zone, FR pages for the FR zone**; Auto-group.
- **Two pricing zones (one EN, one FR)**; add all stores to both.
- Ensure all sessions have run; **Hidden in Hosted**; no theme.
- Key Message / Sale Story — **EN: "Weekly Flyer"; FR: "Circulaire hebdomadaire".**
- Begin Setup QC.

### Setup QC (owned by Vendor)
- Let sessions run; check geography; **Standard 4 thumbnails (ensure logo is captured)**; Sale Story "Weekly Flyer / Circulaire hebdomadaire"; no theme; mark Setup QC complete.

## ⚠️ Common errors / risk items
- **Availability toggles:** Flipp only = **Available on Flipp & Distribution, Hidden on Hosted.**
- **Look for multiple products** — box each separately.

## QC specifics
- **Box Draw (Low; Auto-Box ON, Box QC bot ON):** **Exclude** coupons, packaged deals, retailer logo, sign-up page, social media, special weblinks.
- **Tag / Tag QC (Low; Auto-tag ON):** Brand box-draw/QC specific. **Include** name, pre/postfix, description, price; **exclude** valid dates, SKU, sale story, categories, disclaimer, original price, URLs.
  - Name tagged per the PDF (e.g. "BANANAS"; French "BLEUETS OU FRAMBOISES"). Price per PDF with postfix (e.g. 0.49 + "lb", or prefix "2 for" / current price "4").
  - **Brand** appears under the product name in quotation marks (e.g. Name "COOKED TURKEY BREAST", Brand "SCHNEIDERS"); pick the best dropdown match.
- **Image QC:** **Not required.**
- **Spotchecks:** reference Tag/Tag QC instructions.

## Post-processing (owned by DOC)
- Item Category, Item Image, URL/Links, SKU, and Ad-hoc QC not detailed in the OneGuide (placeholders only).

## FQC (owned by DOC)
- Standard FQC; mark Autostack spotcheck complete; dates correct per first page; all items tagged per their name on the PDF; Standard 4 thumbnails; no geography changes; final FQC.
